FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does reducing the PDF size hurt quality?

A smaller file always means some loss of sharpness. Haebom finds the highest quality that stays within your limit and lowers the resolution only when it has to. Documents read on screen are usually fine.

How do I resize a PDF to 100KB, 200KB or 500KB?

Add your PDF, pick the size limit (100KB to 5MB, or type your own) and press Resize PDF. For documents with many pages or colors, choose Grayscale to get a smaller file.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens inside your browser and the file is never sent anywhere. That makes it safe for ID copies and supporting documents.

Can I copy or search text in the resized PDF?

No. Each page is converted to an image and compressed, so the text can no longer be selected, copied or searched. It suits files you only submit, and scans. Keep the original if you need the text.

How are KB and MB counted?

Some portals count 1KB as 1,000 bytes and others as 1,024. Haebom uses 1,000 bytes per KB, so the result stays under the limit either way. 1MB is 1,000KB.

What if it says it could not get under my limit?

A document with many pages and a very small limit is hard to fit. Raise the limit a little, choose Grayscale, or split the PDF and resize the parts.

Does it work with password-protected PDFs?

No. A password-protected PDF cannot be opened. Please remove the password first.

How many pages can it handle?

To protect your browser's memory, up to 60 pages per file. For longer documents, split them first.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Pick a PDF from your phone's files, resize it and save the result directly.
